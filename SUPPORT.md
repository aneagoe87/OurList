## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I create a shopping list?
Tap the **+** button on the Lists tab. Give your list a name, optionally set a budget category and store, then tap **Create**.

### How do I share lists with family members?
OurList syncs automatically with family members who use OurBudget. Once a family is set up in OurBudget, all shopping lists, products, and stores sync in real time across all devices.

### How do I scan barcodes?
Open a shopping list, tap **Add Item**, then tap **Scan Barcode**. Point your camera at the product's barcode. OurList will look up the product and auto-fill the details.

### How do I delete my account?
Go to **Settings** > **Delete Account & Data**. This permanently removes all your shopping lists, products, stores, and account data from your device and iCloud.

### How do I restore my purchases?
Go to **Settings** > **Restore Purchases**. Your Pro subscription will be restored if previously purchased.

### What platforms does OurList support?
OurList is available natively on **iPhone**, **iPad**, and **Mac**. Data syncs across all your devices via iCloud.

### How does OurList work with OurBudget?
OurList reads your family's budget categories from OurBudget. When you check out a shopping list, transactions are automatically logged to your family budget. Both apps share the same iCloud data.

### My data isn't syncing between devices
1. Make sure you're signed into the **same iCloud account** on all devices
2. Check that iCloud Drive is enabled in System Settings
3. Go to **Settings** > **CloudKit Diagnostics** and verify Store Mode shows "CloudKit"
4. Tap **Push Local Changes** to force a sync
